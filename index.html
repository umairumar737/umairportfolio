The math

How steps become miles

Behind the calculator is a simple stride-based formula — the same approach fitness researchers use to estimate walking distance.

1

Estimate your stride length. Multiply your height by 0.41 (women) or 0.415 (men) to get one stride in inches.

2

Find your steps per mile. Divide 63,360 (inches in a mile) by your stride length.

3

Convert. miles = steps ÷ steps per mile — and km = miles × 1.60934.

Everyone's stride is a little different, so treat results as a close estimate rather than an exact measurement. Pace, terrain and footwear all nudge the real number.
